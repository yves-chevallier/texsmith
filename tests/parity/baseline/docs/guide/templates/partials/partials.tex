\title{Contract macros}
\author{}\date{}
\begin{document}
\maketitle
The \LaTeX{} body of a document comes from tmark's writer, which emits a
fixed macro or environment per construct. Structural constructs (emphasis,
lists, headings, figures, tables, links, footnotes) are plain \LaTeX{} the
writer owns; the constructs a template may want to restyle are \textbf{contract
macros} provided by the \tscodeinline{ts-\allowbreak{}*} fragments. The writer names the fragment in
\tscodeinline{Requires.fragments}; the fragment defines the macros; a template restyles a
construct by redefining its macro.

The table of contracts is \tscodeinline{tmark.fragments()} (\tscodeinline{FRAGMENTS} in
\tscodeinline{tmark\_ir::registry}); the names follow one convention: macros \tscodeinline{\textbackslash{}ts<name>},
environments \tscodeinline{ts<name>}, at most one optional keyval group first, content
last. Keys are parsed with \tscodeinline{pgfkeys} under \tscodeinline{/ts/<name>/} and unknown keys
are ignored, so an older fragment survives a newer writer.

Identity: for a contract macro the writer passes \tscodeinline{id=}; the fragment places
\tscodeinline{\textbackslash{}phantomsection\textbackslash{}label}. Attributes of a container are forwarded as keys
(\tscodeinline{\#id} \tsscript{symbols}{\(\rightarrow\) }\tscodeinline{id}, classes \tsscript{symbols}{\(\rightarrow\) }\tscodeinline{class=\{a,b\}}, \tscodeinline{key=val} as is); \tscodeinline{lang} and \tscodeinline{media}
never are.

The Typst writer emits one hyphenated function per contract (\tscodeinline{\#ts-\allowbreak{}callout},
\tscodeinline{\#ts-\allowbreak{}code}, \tscodeinline{\#ts-\allowbreak{}keys}, ...) with the same argument names, defined in
\tscodeinline{src/texsmith/templates/common/texsmith.typ}, copied next to the \tscodeinline{.typ}.
\section{Overriding a construct}
Three levels, in the template's \tscodeinline{.tex} after \tscodeinline{\textbackslash{}VAR\{extra\_packages\}} (the
fragments are \tscodeinline{\textbackslash{}usepackage}d there):
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Restyle through the pgfkeys family.} \tscodeinline{tscallout} and \tscodeinline{tscode} are
\tscodeinline{tcolorbox} boxes; the family style is appended to their options, and a
class named in \tscodeinline{class=\{…\}} applies \tscodeinline{/ts/<name>/class/<class>} when it is
defined:
\begin{tscode}[lang=latex, engine=pygments]
\begin{Verbatim}[commandchars=\\\{\},breaklines, breakanywhere, commandchars=\\\{\}]
\PY{k}{\PYZbs{}tcbset}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZob{}}/ts/code/.append style=\PY{n+nb}{\PYZob{}}frame hidden, boxrule=0pt\PY{n+nb}{\PYZcb{}}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZcb{}}
\PY{k}{\PYZbs{}tcbset}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZob{}}/ts/callout/class/wide/.style=\PY{n+nb}{\PYZob{}}grow to left by=2cm\PY{n+nb}{\PYZcb{}}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZcb{}}
\end{Verbatim}
\end{tscode}
\item \textbf{Redefine the macro, same signature.}
\begin{tscode}[lang=latex, engine=pygments]
\begin{Verbatim}[commandchars=\\\{\},breaklines, breakanywhere, commandchars=\\\{\}]
\PY{k}{\PYZbs{}RenewDocumentCommand}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZob{}}\PY{k}{\PYZbs{}tscodeinline}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZcb{}}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZob{}}O\PY{n+nb}{\PYZob{}}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZcb{}}m\PY{n+nb}{\PYZcb{}}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZob{}}\PY{k}{\PYZbs{}mbox}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZob{}}\PY{k}{\PYZbs{}texttt}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZob{}}\PYZsh{}2\PY{n+nb}{\PYZcb{}}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZcb{}}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZcb{}}
\PY{k}{\PYZbs{}RenewDocumentCommand}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZob{}}\PY{k}{\PYZbs{}tsdivider}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZcb{}}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZob{}}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZcb{}}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZob{}}\PY{k}{\PYZbs{}bigskip}\PY{k}{\PYZbs{}hrule}\PY{k}{\PYZbs{}bigskip}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZcb{}}
\PY{k}{\PYZbs{}pgfkeys}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZob{}}/ts/rule/.cd, thickness=1pt, width=0.4\PY{k}{\PYZbs{}linewidth}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZcb{}}
\PY{k}{\PYZbs{}RenewDocumentEnvironment}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZob{}}tsdiv@multicolumn\PY{n+nb}{\PYZcb{}}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZob{}}O\PY{n+nb}{\PYZob{}}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZcb{}}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZcb{}}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZob{}}\PY{k}{\PYZbs{}begin}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZob{}}multicols\PY{n+nb}{\PYZcb{}}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZob{}}3\PY{n+nb}{\PYZcb{}}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZcb{}}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZob{}}\PY{k}{\PYZbs{}end}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZob{}}multicols\PY{n+nb}{\PYZcb{}}\PY{n+nb}{\PYZcb{}}
\end{Verbatim}
\end{tscode}
A custom \tscodeinline{::: name} container is a \tscodeinline{tsdiv@name} environment defined the
same way (one optional argument, the raw key list; the parsed keys are in
\tscodeinline{\textbackslash{}ts@div@cols}, \tscodeinline{\textbackslash{}ts@div@title}, \tscodeinline{\textbackslash{}ts@div@id}).
\item \textbf{Replace the fragment.} \tscodeinline{press.fragments: \{disable: [ts-\allowbreak{}code], append:
[./my-\allowbreak{}code.sty]\}}; the replacement must define every \tscodeinline{provides} entry of
its row in \tscodeinline{tmark.fragments()}.
\end{enumerate}
Guard a redefinition when the fragment is conditional (\tscodeinline{ts-\allowbreak{}code} only loads
when the document has code): \tscodeinline{\textbackslash{}ifcsname tscodeinline\textbackslash{}endcsname … \textbackslash{}fi}.
\section{Former partials}
Until 0.6 the \LaTeX{} came from Jinja partials under
\tscodeinline{src/texsmith/adapters/latex/partials}, overridable per template
(\tscodeinline{latex.template.override}) and per fragment (\tscodeinline{partials},
\tscodeinline{required\_partials}). The partials and all three hooks are gone in 0.8.0: the
writer names a macro and a fragment defines it. The mapping:
