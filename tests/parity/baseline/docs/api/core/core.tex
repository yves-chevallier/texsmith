\section{Diagnostics emitters}
\tscodeinline{texsmith.core.diagnostics} defines the \tscodeinline{DiagnosticEmitter} protocol plus a few
stock implementations. Pass any emitter into \tscodeinline{ConversionService},
\tscodeinline{convert\_documents}, or \tscodeinline{TemplateSession} to intercept warnings, errors, and
structured events.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X}
\toprule
\textbf{Emitter} & \textbf{Description} & \textbf{Typical usage} \\
\midrule
\tscodeinline{CliEmitter} (\tscodeinline{texsmith.ui.cli.diagnostics}) & Rich-powered emitter used by the Typer CLI. Respects \tscodeinline{-\allowbreak{}v} and \tscodeinline{-\allowbreak{}-\allowbreak{}debug}, paints warnings as panels, and streams structured events to the diagnostics sidebar. & Default when running \tscodeinline{texsmith}. Import it in automation scripts when you want human-friendly output. \\
\tscodeinline{LoggingEmitter} & Forwards \tscodeinline{warning}, \tscodeinline{error}, and \tscodeinline{event} calls to the standard \tscodeinline{logging} module. & Daemons, notebooks, or services that rely on existing logging policy. \\
\tscodeinline{NullEmitter} & No-op implementation. Useful when you want silent conversions or plan to capture diagnostics out-of-band. & Unit tests and benchmarking. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
Emitters expose a \tscodeinline{debug\_enabled} flag so downstream handlers can decide whether
to include stack traces or expensive state dumps. Implement your own to route
diagnostics to metrics systems or structured loggers.
