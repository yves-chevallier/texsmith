\section{Inline Math}\label{inline-math}

Inline math uses the usual delimiters \texttt{\textbackslash{}( ... \textbackslash{})} or \texttt{\$ ... \$}:

\begin{code}{markdown}{}{}
\begin{Verbatim}[commandchars=\\\{\},breaklines, breakanywhere, commandchars=\\\{\}]
The quadratic formula is given by \PYZbs{}(x = \PYZbs{}frac\PYZob{}\PYZhy{}b \PYZbs{}pm \PYZbs{}sqrt\PYZob{}b\PYZca{}2 \PYZhy{} 4ac\PYZcb{}\PYZcb{}\PYZob{}2a\PYZcb{}\PYZbs{})
or \PYZdl{}x = \PYZbs{}frac\PYZob{}\PYZhy{}b \PYZbs{}pm \PYZbs{}sqrt\PYZob{}b\PYZca{}2 \PYZhy{} 4ac\PYZcb{}\PYZcb{}\PYZob{}2a\PYZcb{}\PYZdl{}.
\end{Verbatim}
\end{code}
Rendered as:

\begin{displayquote}

The quadratic formula is given by $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$
or $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$.

\end{displayquote}
\begin{callout}[callout note]{Note}
Skip the spaces right after \texttt{\$} or \texttt{\textbackslash{}(}---they confuse the parser.
\end{callout}
