\chapter{Deep nesting}
Markdown nests a list as deeply as its author indents it. \LaTeX{} stops at four
levels of \tscodeinline{itemize}, four of \tscodeinline{enumerate} and six lists of any kind inside one
another, and says \tscodeinline{Too deeply nested}. The \tscodeinline{texsmith-\allowbreak{}lists} package the
templates share lifts both limits to nine levels; this page walks to six.
